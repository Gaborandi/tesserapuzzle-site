How it works
  1. The numbers never move. You color the tiles.
  2. Each region must contain six tiles with 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 once each.
  3. Tiles in the same region must join along their edges. Corners alone do not count.
  4. Choose a color, then tap tiles. Tap a tile again with its color selected to clear it. The starting tiles stay fixed.

Keyboard: Tab to the board; arrow keys move between tiles; Enter or Space colors a tile. Letters A–F select a region after the first move.
