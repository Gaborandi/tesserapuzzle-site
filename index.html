The core idea

One simple action. Three ways to think.

Choose a region, then fill it with neighbouring cells. Each region must stay connected and contain the right number of cells. The rule changes with the mode.

01Logic

Keep every number unique

The numbers are already placed. Draw equal-size, connected regions with no repeated numbers.

02Chroma

Match the colour recipe

Connect red, yellow and blue cells in the proportions shown by each region’s recipe.

03Equate

Make the numbers work

Connect numbers to meet each region’s arithmetic target. Equate unlocks as you progress.
