Neon Eye

Follow the needle and tap while it crosses the glowing arc. Land as many hits as you can in six attempts.

The timing round

Attempt 1 of 60 hits

Use the button, Enter or Space. Each attempt starts when you are ready.

How to play

Tap when the white needle lines up with the glowing arc. After each attempt, the needle holds its position so you can see your timing.
