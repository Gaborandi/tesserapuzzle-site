Lapsewood

Wake the garden in three taps. Age a cloud into rain and watch it grow the seeds that touch it along an edge.

After the rain

3 taps left0 of 4 plants growing

Grow all four seeds into sprouts, bushes or trees.

How to play

Each tap advances the tile. Rain grows neighboring seeds, sprouts and bushes. A seed beyond the rain can be tapped directly. Undo restores a tap and its whole chain.
