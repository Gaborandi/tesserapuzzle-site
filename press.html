The full mobile game

  • Logic, colour mixing in Chroma, and arithmetic targets in Equate.
  • 600 Journey levels: 200 per type, from 4×4 to 8×8; plus Daily and Weekly challenges.
  • Free with banner and occasional between-puzzle ads, optional rewarded ads, a one-time Premium purchase that removes ads, and optional hint packs. No subscription or game account.
  • Android, iPhone and iPad. Availability and prices vary by storefront; Google Play is not available in Indonesia or Vietnam.
